How to read this

Fees are annual tuition only, for the stated academic year, and exclude admission fees, deposits, transport and materials unless noted. Schools publish these inconsistently; a ~ marks a low-confidence figure and an em dash means we could not evidence one rather than that the school is cheap.

Play is a 1–5 score for sport and play infrastructure actually built on that campus — 5 means multiple full-size grounds plus pool and indoor complex, 1 means little more than a play area. Olympiad counts only results verifiable in official tables — INOI/IOITC/IOI via IARCS, INMO, NTSE, or genuine national representation with student, school and year on record. Mass-participation contests marketed as “olympiads” (SOF, Unified Council, SilverZone) are excluded, so most schools show a dash and that is not a criticism. Board-topper counts were researched and then dropped: CBSE publishes no merit list and the IB does not release results to third parties, so such a column would rank a board’s disclosure policy rather than a school.

Signal — This column is a screening signal based only on public, campus-specific documented incidents and clearly labelled, unverified community reports; it is not a prediction that a child will be unsafe or a finding of wrongdoing. ‘Insufficient evidence’ means the public record did not support a reliable rating—not that the school is safe or unsafe—and larger or better-known schools may attract more reports simply because they are more visible.
