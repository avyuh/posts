How to read this

School run is an estimated weekday 7:30-8:30am driving time to the nearest qualifying school — peak traffic, not free-flow. School km is straight-line. The two do not track each other: a 2.7 km Whitefield hop can take 30 min while a 4.7 km run north of the city takes 16, which is why the table sorts by time.

Metro km is aerial distance to the nearest station carrying passengers in August 2026 — Purple, Green and Yellow only. The ORR/Blue line (Ibbalur, Ecospace, Bellandur, Marathahalli) and Pink are not open; the sanctioned Sarjapur–Hebbal line is approved-only with a 2033 target. No planned station is counted, which is why Sarjapur rows show large metro distances.

“Good school” means established, recognised board (CBSE/ICSE/IB/IGCSE), real Grades 1–10 at that campus — not a quality ranking. A ~ marks a low-confidence row.

Price caveats. Carryover prices were verified April 2026; newly researched ones August 2026 — two vintages in one column. Godrej Lake Gardens is low-confidence: sources span roughly ₹12,850–₹16,050/sq ft. Sterling Brookside has no evidenced price and shows an em dash. Amenities at under-construction phases are as-promised, not as-built.
