### Legend

- **Amenity Tier** — Play/sports infrastructure quality (Tier 1 = strongest, Tier 3 = needs verification)
- **Metro Bucket** — #1–12 are "Strong 12" (solid metro fit), #13–15 are "Hold 3" (weaker metro)
- **ETV Distance** — Straight-line distance to Embassy Tech Village, Kadubeesanahalli
- **Adjacent** — Project boundary touches or nearly touches metro station

### Best picks by category

- **Best overall balance** (metro + office + play): Mahindra Blossom, Brigade Lakefront, Purva Westend
- **Best play amenities**: Prestige Jindal City (Tier 1 — cricket, skating, tennis, basketball, mini soccer)
- **Best locality**: Brigade Gateway (Rajajinagar) — but ₹21k/sqft
- **Best value**: Brigade Panorama (₹10.1k) & Brigade Millennium (₹10.8k)
- **Closest to office**: Brigade Lakefront (5.9 km), Purva Westend (7.2 km), TVS Emerald Jardin (8.1 km)
